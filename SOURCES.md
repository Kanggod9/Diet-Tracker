# Primary sources

Retrieved 2026-08-11 unless an effective/version date is stated.

- Android: [NutritionRecord](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/health/connect/client/records/NutritionRecord), [HydrationRecord](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/health/connect/client/records/HydrationRecord), [HealthPermission](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/health/connect/client/permission/HealthPermission), and [Health Connect releases](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/health-connect).
- USDA FoodData Central: [API guide](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/) and [data documentation](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/data-documentation/). Requests and returned records are restricted to Foundation and SR Legacy.
- US FDA: [Daily Values](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/daily-value-nutrition-and-supplement-facts-labels), used as general adult label references rather than personal targets.
- European Union: Regulation 1169/2011 [consolidated text effective 2025-04-01](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02011R1169-20250401), Annex XIII.
- Singapore HealthHub: [adult dietary guidelines](https://www.healthhub.sg/well-being-and-lifestyle/food-diet-and-nutrition/dietary_guidelines_adults) and [RDA overview](https://www.healthhub.sg/well-being-and-lifestyle/food-diet-and-nutrition/recommended_dietary_allowances).
- OpenAI: [Responses API](https://platform.openai.com/docs/api-reference/responses/create), [images and vision](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/images-vision), [API key safety](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/5112595-best-practices-for-api-key-safety), and [ChatGPT/API billing separation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8156019).

Where a profile lacks an authoritative exact threshold, the app leaves it unavailable. Singapore sugar guidance uses the distinct `ADDED_SUGAR` field only; total sugar is never substituted for added/free sugar.
